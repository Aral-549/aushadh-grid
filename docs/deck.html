Shortages, waste and bad data at the same time, in the same district.

Stock-outs next to surplusOne PHC turns patients away while another 40 km away holds months of the same medicine. Nobody can see both.
Medicine expires on the shelfQuiet centres hold lots that expire unused, while busy centres nearby are short of the same item.
Bad data, bad forecastsA PHC that ran out records zero use, so models learn that demand is low. Late reports and typos add to the error.

The solution

One console for every PHC: signals, stock, expiry, transfers, beds and staff.

  • Outbreak signals from clinic visit counts feed straight into the demand forecast
  • Forecasts that survive messy data: stock-out days, typos and late reports are handled
  • Expiry simulation of every lot, first-expiry-first-out
  • Transfer orders that prevent stock-outs and rescue expiring stock, cold-chain aware
  • SMS both ways: stock reports in with typo checks, work orders out to every pharmacist

  • Referral finder, federated BRICS model and Gemini briefs in any language

1 · See the outbreak coming

Clinic visit data triggers outbreak demand for exactly the affected districts.

Daily fever, diarrhoea and heat-illness OPD counts per district are checked with the CDC EARS C2 method. The baseline is 7 days ending 2 days back, and an alert needs 3 SD above it on 2 of the last 3 days.

fever visits in Srikakulam vs the 4-week baseline, 4 days above threshold. Vizianagaram alerts too; Visakhapatnam is on watch.

One click applies outbreak demand to those three districts. Critical medicine lines rise from 50 to 61, and transfers are re-planned at once.

Aushadh Grid04
2 · Forecasts that survive real data

Stock-out days are not zero demand. The model knows the difference.

  • Censored demand: 3+ zero days at a PHC that normally dispenses an item are re-estimated. The pilot restores 298 stock-out days.
  • Entry errors: days above 4× their local median are capped (27 caught). SMS jumps of more than 60 days of demand are held for confirmation.
  • Late reports: today's stock is estimated from forecast use, and uncertainty widens with report age.
  • Forecast: exponential smoothing with weekday seasonality, tuned on an earlier window. On a 14-day holdout it misses 18.9% of actual use vs 21.3% for a 28-day average, and 19.7% vs 24.3% on lines that recently ran out.

BRICS cooperation · federated learning

Countries share what the model learned, never the patient data.

Each national node trains on its own PHC records. A coordinator averages the weights (FedAvg) and returns a global model.
