## Brief description

```
Aushadh Grid is a federated AI platform for PHC medicine supply chains. Syndromic OPD surveillance (CDC EARS C2) spots outbreaks before declaration and switches forecasts to outbreak demand for the affected districts. Demand for 528 medicine lines at 48 pilot PHCs is forecast from cleaned history (stock-out days re-estimated, typos capped, stale reports adjusted); on a 14-day holdout it misses 18.9% of use vs 21.3% for a 28-day average. A cold-chain-aware optimiser moves surplus to PHCs about to run out and rescues expiring lots; a day-by-day simulation shows it cuts vital-medicine stock-out days from 179 to 16. Transfers become vehicle runs (47% fewer road km) and every pharmacist gets an SMS work order in the codes they report with. Warehouse indents cover the rest, vital first. BRICS nodes train a shared model with FedAvg, sharing only weights. Gemini writes grounded briefs in any language. Open source, runs in a browser. Synthetic data.
```
