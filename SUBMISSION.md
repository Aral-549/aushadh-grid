## Brief description

```
Aushadh Grid is a federated AI platform that makes PHC medicine supply chains prepared, not just reactive. It stress-tests every district against dengue, diarrhoea and heatwave surges: pilot PHCs could meet 84% of demand for vital medicines in their worst emergency. It pre-positions spare stock in calm times, runs each district as one shared shelf through a hub (half the safety stock), and prices the hub buffers that lift every district above 90% (Rs 1.96 lakh for 48 PHCs). Day to day, EARS C2 surveillance flags outbreaks early, a backtested forecast (18.9% error vs 21.3% for a 28-day average) covers 528 medicine lines, and a cold-chain-aware optimiser cuts vital stock-out days from 179 to 16, as vehicle runs with SMS work orders for pharmacists. BRICS nodes share a model via FedAvg, never data. Gemini writes grounded briefs in any language. Open source, runs in a browser. Synthetic data.
```
